conditions.

## Dataset801 ATLAS-derived model checkpoints

`Dataset801_ATLAS_final_checkpoints.tar` contains trained model parameters
derived from use of the ATLAS dataset.

The ATLAS source dataset is distributed under Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

To provide a conservative and source-compatible redistribution policy, the
Dataset801 trained-checkpoint archive is made available under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Users must also cite the original ATLAS dataset:

Quinton F, Popoff R, Presles B, et al.
A Tumor and Liver Automatic Segmentation (ATLAS) Dataset:
A Hepatocellular Carcinoma Dataset for Automatic Segmentation of Liver and
Liver Tumours on Contrast-Enhanced Magnetic Resonance Imaging.
Data. 2023;8(5):79.

## Dataset804 and Dataset805 OpenSwissHCC-derived checkpoints

`Dataset804_venous_primary_checkpoints.tar` and
`Dataset805_arterial_venous_primary_checkpoints.tar`
contain trained model parameters derived from the OpenSwissHCC dataset.

The OpenSwissHCC v1 record used for this study is:
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17517079

The source record is indexed as CC BY. The derived Dataset804 and Dataset805
checkpoint archives in this release are made available under CC BY 4.0, with
attribution to the original OpenSwissHCC dataset required.

## Source medical images

No raw ATLAS, OpenSwissHCC, LiverHccSeg or HCC-TACE-Seg medical images are
redistributed in this repository or release.

Users must obtain the source datasets independently and comply with the
licence and access terms of each original dataset.
